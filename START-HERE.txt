Thank you for your purchase! Here's everything you need in 2 minutes.


▶ RUN IT (no install)
---------------------
Just double-click  index.html  — it opens in your browser and works
completely offline. That's the whole "install."

First time? Click "Explore with sample data" to see everything filled
in, then clear it and add your own.


▶ THE THREE TABS
----------------
• Dashboard      — 19 metrics with goals & progress
• Sales Pipeline — a full CRM for every lead
• Reports        — charts + a built-in advisor that tells you what to fix

Press the "?" key anytime for keyboard shortcuts, or click Help
(top-right) for the full guide and a guided tour.


▶ IMPORTANT: BACK UP YOUR DATA
------------------------------
Everything is stored privately in YOUR browser. That means it's yours
alone — but clearing your browser erases it. Once a month:

  Settings → Backup → export the two JSON files.

Or set up free Google Sheets sync (Settings → Google Sheets sync) for
automatic backup + phone access. Full steps in Help.


▶ MAKE IT YOURS
---------------
Settings → Appearance:
  • Pick a theme (Midnight / Crème / Ink & Gold)
  • Set your company name + tagline
  • Upload your logo (with a crop tool)


▶ PUT IT ONLINE (optional, ~5 min, free)
----------------------------------------
Want your own private URL you can open from any device?
  1. Go to netlify.com (free account)
  2. Drag this whole folder onto the "deploy" area
  3. You get a live link instantly. Bookmark it.
(Your data still stays private in each browser you use it on.)
